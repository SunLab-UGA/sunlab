Research

research directions in wireless communications, sensing, security, and edge learning.

SunLab studies wireless communication and networking systems for 5G and beyond. The lab combines theory, measurement, simulation, and prototyping, with a particular focus on radio-aware AI, edge computing, sensing, IoT, and secure cyber-physical systems.

Research Areas

Radio Radiance Field

3D wireless channel representation with visual cues and sparse wireless samples.

mmWave Communication for CAVs

Side-information assisted beam management for connected and automated vehicles.

Ultra-Low-Power IoT

Cross-layer design for energy- and spectrum-efficient large-scale IoT connectivity.

5G and Beyond Radio Access

PHY-layer methods for spectral, energy, and computation efficiency.

Edge Computing and Learning

Wireless resource allocation for mobile edge computing and distributed learning.

Wireless Sensing

RF-based localization, monitoring, and environmental sensing.

Wireless and CPS Security

Physical-layer security and embedded system security for cyber-physical systems.
