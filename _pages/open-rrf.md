# Open RRF

SunLab is developing open resources for radio radiance fields and spatial wireless channel representation. These projects explore how visual reconstruction methods, wireless measurements, and physics-guided models can represent full radio propagation paths.

## Featured Links

- [RF-PGS](https://github.com/SunLab-UGA/RF-PGS): fully structured spatial wireless channel representation based on Planar Gaussian Splatting.
- [RF-3DGS](https://github.com/SunLab-UGA/RF-3DGS): radio radiance field reconstruction using 3D Gaussian Splatting.
- [Visual Reconstruction Viewer](https://sunlab.uga.edu/RF-3DGS/main.html)
- [Radio Reconstruction Viewer](https://sunlab.uga.edu/RF-3DGS/RF-3DGS-RRF/main.html)
- [Visual Reconstruction of UWM Lab](https://sunlab.uga.edu/RF-3DGS/RF-3DGS-UWM/main.html)
- [Radio Reconstruction of UWM Lab](https://sunlab.uga.edu/RF-3DGS/RF-3DGS-UWM-RRF/main.html)
