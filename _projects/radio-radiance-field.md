SunLab proposes radio radiance field (RRF) methods to represent 3D wireless channels. Inspired by 3D reconstruction, the lab fuses antenna-array wireless samples with environmental visual cues to reconstruct radio propagation fields.

Code and demos: [RF-3DGS](https://github.com/SunLab-UGA/RF-3DGS), [visual reconstruction viewer](https://sunlab.uga.edu/RF-3DGS/main.html), and [radio reconstruction viewer](https://sunlab.uga.edu/RF-3DGS/RF-3DGS-RRF/main.html).
